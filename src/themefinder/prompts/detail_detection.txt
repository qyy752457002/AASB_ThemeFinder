{system_prompt}

You are analyzing organizational submissions to the AASB ED SR1 Consultation.

This stage is DETAIL (EVIDENCE) DETECTION.
Your task is to determine whether each response contains
SUBSTANTIVE, POLICY-RELEVANT DETAIL
that would be useful to regulators when drafting or revising standards.

This is NOT a sentiment task and NOT a theme task.
It is a SIGNAL QUALITY FILTER.

==================================================
TASK OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Given:
• ONE consultation QUESTION
• A list of FREE-TEXT RESPONSES (with response_id)

Your task is to assess EACH response and decide whether it contains
RICH, SUBSTANTIVE EVIDENCE
as defined below.

You MUST include every response_id in the output.

==================================================
DEFINITION: EVIDENCE-RICH RESPONSE
==================================================

A response should be classified as EVIDENCE_RICH = YES
ONLY IF it satisfies BOTH conditions:

--------------------------------------------------
1. Question-Relevant Substance
--------------------------------------------------

The response:
• Clearly addresses the question being asked, AND
• Provides reasoning, explanation, or insight that goes beyond
  a generic opinion or simple agreement/disagreement.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Concrete Supporting Detail
--------------------------------------------------

The response contains AT LEAST ONE of the following:

• Specific and verifiable references
  – named legislation, standards, frameworks, or schemes
    (e.g. IFRS S1/S2, NGER Scheme, GHG Protocol)
  – reports, consultations, studies, or regulatory documents
  – dates, thresholds, classifications, or technical requirements

• Concrete illustrative examples
  – clear examples of how a requirement would operate in practice
  – entity-type–specific scenarios (e.g. NFPs, superannuation funds,
    public sector entities)
  – implementation, assurance, or operational impacts explained clearly

• Contextualised professional or organisational experience
  – explanation grounded in organisational role, sector, or reporting context
  – includes sufficient background to explain *why* the experience
    is relevant to policy design

==================================================
WHAT IS NOT EVIDENCE-RICH
==================================================

Classify EVIDENCE_RICH = NO if the response:

• Merely states agreement or disagreement without explanation
• Uses high-level or generic language with no concrete support
• Repeats commonly expected concerns (e.g. "costs will increase")
  without elaboration
• Makes broad assertions without context or justification
• Is empty, procedural, or non-substantive

==================================================
DECISION TEST
==================================================

Before assigning YES, ask:

“Could this response be reasonably cited, summarised,
or relied upon in a regulatory discussion paper,
consultation summary, or Board briefing —
above and beyond what is already obvious?”

If the answer is NO → EVIDENCE_RICH = NO

==================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT (MANDATORY)
==================================================

For EACH response, output exactly:

response_id: <id>
EVIDENCE_RICH: <YES | NO>

Rules:
• Every response_id MUST appear exactly once
• Do NOT add explanations or commentary
• Do NOT omit empty responses
• Use ONLY 'YES' or 'NO'
