You are analyzing organizational submissions to the AASB ED SR1 Consultation.

This stage is THEME GENERATION.
Sentiment / POSITION classification has already been completed in a prior step.
Your task here is NOT to determine agreement or disagreement, but to extract
the substantive themes expressed in responses to a single consultation question.

==================================================
TASK OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Given:
• ONE consultation QUESTION
• A list of RESPONSES to that question (from multiple organisations)

Your task is to identify INITIAL THEMES such that:

1. Each theme represents a **distinct substantive point, concern, rationale, or recommendation**
   expressed in the responses.
2. Themes should reflect **what respondents are talking about**, not how strongly they feel.
3. Every materially different viewpoint or issue raised should be captured by at least one theme.
4. Ignore responses that are irrelevant, generic, or clearly off-topic for the question.
5. Do NOT merge themes aggressively at this stage — it is acceptable for themes to overlap
   slightly. Consolidation will occur in later pipeline stages.
6. Themes must be phrased to be useful for **policy analysis and standard-setting decisions**.

==================================================
OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

For each identified theme, output:

• topic_label  
  - A concise phrase (maximum ~6–8 words)
  - Should clearly signal the core idea of the theme
  - Avoid vague terms like “general concern” or “mixed views”

• topic_description  
  - 1–2 sentences expanding the idea
  - Describe the common reasoning, issue, or recommendation raised
  - Write in neutral, analytical language (no advocacy)

DO NOT:
• assign sentiment or POSITION values  
• reference individual organisations by name  
• quote responses verbatim  
• include examples or evidence excerpts  
• rank or prioritise themes  

==================================================
IMPORTANT CLARIFICATIONS
==================================================

• This stage is EXPLORATORY:
  – Capture all relevant ideas before condensation or refinement
• Empty or non-substantive responses should NOT generate themes
• A single response may inform multiple themes
• Themes should remain question-specific

EXAMPLE (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)
==================================================

QUESTION:
"Do you agree with the proposal to require Scope 3 emissions disclosure?"

RESPONSES:
[
  "Scope 3 data is unreliable and difficult to obtain.",
  "Mandatory Scope 3 disclosure is critical for investor decision-making.",
  "The cost of collecting Scope 3 data would be excessive for smaller entities."
]

POSSIBLE THEMES:
• Data reliability concerns  
  – Respondents raise concerns that Scope 3 emissions data lacks consistency and reliability,
    which could undermine the decision-usefulness of disclosures.

• Investor usefulness of Scope 3 disclosure  
  – Some respondents argue that Scope 3 emissions information is necessary to understand
    full climate-related risk exposure.

• Cost and proportionality issues  
  – Respondents note that mandatory Scope 3 disclosure may impose disproportionate
    compliance costs, particularly for smaller entities.
