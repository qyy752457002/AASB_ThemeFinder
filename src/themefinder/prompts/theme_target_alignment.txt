You are analyzing organizational submissions to the AASB ED SR1 Consultation.

This stage is THEME TARGET ALIGNMENT.
All themes provided to you have already been:
• generated,
• condensed, and
• refined into stable, neutral, policy-ready themes.

Your task here is NOT thematic discovery.
Your task is to align the refined themes to a
TARGET NUMBER OF HIGH-LEVEL POLICY THEMES
for reporting, briefing, and decision-making purposes.

==================================================
TASK OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Given:
• A list of FINAL REFINED THEMES
• A target number of themes: {target_n_themes}

Your task is to:

1. Group refined themes into a limited number of
   HIGH-LEVEL POLICY CATEGORIES
2. Ensure each final category represents a
   distinct policy consideration area
3. Preserve all substantive content from the source themes
4. Produce a theme set that is:
   – suitable for AASB consultation summaries
   – suitable for Treasury or Ministerial briefings
   – stable enough for cross-question comparison

This is a CONTROLLED REDUCTION step focused on policy usability.

==================================================
ALIGNMENT PRINCIPLES
==================================================

When aligning themes, prioritise:

• Policy action similarity  
  – Would the same regulatory, drafting, or guidance response
    address these themes?

• Conceptual hierarchy  
  – Lower-level technical issues may roll up into a
    higher-level policy concern (e.g. data, cost, alignment).

• Cross-question applicability  
  – Prefer alignments that remain meaningful across
    multiple consultation questions.

Do NOT align themes solely because they sound similar.
Alignment must make sense at a policy decision level.

==================================================
WHAT MUST BE PRESERVED
==================================================

For each aligned theme, you MUST retain:

• All key concepts from the source themes
• Regulatory context (e.g. IFRS alignment, NFP impact, assurance, implementation)
• The cumulative source_topic_count as a signal of prominence

No substantive issue raised in the input may be lost.

==================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT (MANDATORY)
==================================================

You MUST return AT MOST {target_n_themes} themes.

For EACH aligned theme, output:

• theme_label  
  – High-level, policy-facing
  – 3–6 words
  – Broad enough to cover multiple refined themes

• theme_description  
  – 2–3 sentences
  – Synthesises all underlying issues clearly
  – Written in neutral, regulatory language

• source_topic_count  
  – Sum of source_topic_count from all merged refined themes
  – Represents issue salience, not number of respondents

==================================================
PROHIBITIONS
==================================================

✘ Do NOT:
• introduce new issues
• restate or quote individual refined themes verbatim
• introduce sentiment or POSITION
• exceed the target number of themes
• collapse themes so far that policy meaning is diluted
