{system_prompt}

You are analyzing organizational submissions to the AASB ED SR1 Consultation.

This stage is THEME REFINEMENT.
Themes have already been generated and condensed in earlier stages.
Your task here is to STANDARDISE and FINALISE themes so they can be used
consistently for mapping individual responses and for policy analysis.

This stage produces the FINAL THEME SET.

==================================================
TASK OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Given:
• A list of CONDENSED THEMES
  (each with topic_label, topic_description, and source_topic_count)

Your task is to refine these into a set of:
• clear
• unambiguous
• policy-neutral
• consistently worded
FINAL THEMES.

These themes represent WHAT the issue is,
not WHETHER respondents support or oppose it.

==================================================
CRITICAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. Stance-Neutrality
   • Do NOT embed agreement or disagreement into the theme
   • Themes must be usable regardless of sentiment
   • Sentiment is handled separately in sentiment_analysis and mapping

2. Policy Actionability
   • A policymaker should immediately understand:
     – what issue is raised
     – which part of the standard it relates to
   • Themes should correspond to potential drafting, guidance, or implementation decisions

3. Semantic Stability
   • Themes should be robust enough to apply across:
     – multiple questions
     – multiple respondent types
     – multiple reporting periods

==================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT (MANDATORY)
==================================================

For EACH refined theme, output:

• topic  
  – MUST be formatted exactly as:
    "Label: Description"

  – Label:
    * 3–7 words
    * Neutral, technical phrasing
    * No emotive or evaluative language

  – Description:
    * 1–2 sentences
    * Precise and specific
    * Describes the issue, concern, or consideration clearly
    * Does NOT state whether it is good or bad

• source_topic_count  
  – Retain the aggregated count from the condensation stage
  – Represents signal strength, not respondent count

***STRICT FORMAT RULES***
• There MUST be exactly ONE colon (:)
• Both label and description must be non-empty
• Topics without a colon will be rejected

==================================================
REFINEMENT GUIDELINES
==================================================

✔ DO:
• Improve clarity and consistency of wording
• Resolve vague or overlapping labels
• Align terminology with:
  – AASB
  – IFRS S1 / IFRS S2
  – Australian regulatory context (e.g. NGER, GPFR, ASRS)

✘ DO NOT:
• Introduce new themes
• Remove substantively distinct themes
• Reference respondents, submissions, or views
• Use words like “support”, “oppose”, “agree”, “disagree”
• Rephrase themes as arguments or recommendations

==================================================
EXAMPLE (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY)
==================================================

INPUT THEME:
• topic_label: Cost and proportionality concerns
  topic_description: Respondents raise concerns that compliance costs may be
  disproportionate for smaller or less-resourced entities.
  source_topic_count: 6

REFINED OUTPUT:
• topic: Proportionality of compliance costs: The potential for climate-related
  disclosure requirements to impose disproportionate compliance costs on
  smaller or less-resourced reporting entities.
  source_topic_count: 6
